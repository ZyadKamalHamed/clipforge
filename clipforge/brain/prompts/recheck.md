## A clip checked again

Each clip here was checked once already, and since then the person making it changed it by hand. They may have moved where it starts or ends, cut words out of it, kept its fillers in, or written its title, its captions or its fact cards themselves. Check it again as it is now, in the same way.

It has one title, the one it goes out with, numbered 0. Give 0 when that title is true to the clip and -1 when it is not.

When words were cut out of the clip, you also get the same stretch as it was said, before the cuts. Viewers hear only the clip. Use the stretch as said to tell whether a cut joins words into something the speaker did not say or did not mean: that is a changed meaning.

A clip heard "fillers and all" keeps its fillers, so none were taken out of it.
