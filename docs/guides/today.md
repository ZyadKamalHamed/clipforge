# Today

Today is ClipForge's home page and the one screen your 15 minutes a day happen on. Each morning it puts everything that waits on you in one list, the item that could earn the most at the top, with a time estimate beside each, and one notification says the list is ready. Everything the list asks for can be done on this page or one click from it. Nothing posts without your approval, as everywhere else in ClipForge.

With no clipping channels or campaigns yet and the path turned off, Today shows Create's drop zone and a **New clipping channel** button instead.

## What ClipForge never does

ClipForge never submits a post to a marketplace, never withdraws money and never presses Post or Schedule on TikTok for an assisted post. Submitting, withdrawing and that last click are yours: ClipForge copies the link, opens the right page in your default browser and fills in what it can, and you do the rest on the platform's or the marketplace's own page. Every earnings figure on Today is ClipForge's estimate from the rates and views you entered, not the marketplace's numbers.

## The page, top to bottom

**The header** says how many of your 15 minutes are left ("12 of 15 minutes left", counting the time your timed items took, a minute begun counting as a whole one) and how long the rest of the list should take.

**Your path to first money** sits above To do until you have walked it. It is six levels: **Set up** (notifications and the background service), **Your first account** (make it, log it in, press Check, set its niche tags), **Your first campaign** (pick one from the briefs and join it), **Your first post** (make clips, post the first one assisted, submit it), **Your first money** (enter its views, see it approved, get paid) and **Your daily machine** (a clipping channel, Instagram through Facebook, a trending sound Reel, a trial reel, a second TikTok account and one real morning). The card shows the level you are on, the steps done out of 22, what you have earned, posts live and accounts, and each level's steps: done, on your list, coming onto your list when it's due (a step another item stands for, like submitting a post), or locked until the steps before it are done. The level you are on is open, and so is any level with a step on your list.

Each open step is an item on To do, with what the account it leads to could earn a day ("Opens about US$5.00 a day (a guess)"), its own time estimate and a button to the page its work is on. ClipForge ticks a step itself when it sees the work done (an account linked, a Check that passed, a campaign made, clips and a post for that campaign, a submission paid); notifications, the live Instagram cap and one real morning you tick by hand. A step done stays done. The steps come after the day's money work, since a submit or a decision can't wait a day and a step can, and they are the first to go **Optional** when the list runs past 15 minutes, so a longer train ride always has something left to do. They never lower the drafts ClipForge makes for tomorrow.

Your first post is assisted: ClipForge fills TikTok in with TikTok's own schedule set to the post's time, and you press **Schedule** in TikTok yourself (**Post**, when it's due now). The step ticks once the post is live.

The account under **Your first account** on the card is chosen from the live briefs: when one niche holds most of the money (40% or more), a clips account for that niche; when the money is spread out, a broad one like Late Night Clips, tagged with the richest niches. The card gives three name ideas, a bio, a profile picture idea and the niche tags. ClipForge never makes the account: you sign up in the TikTok app. Once you tick Make your first account, its name ideas, bio and tags stay put while the briefs change; the second account still follows the live briefs.

To hide the path, turn off **Show the path to your first money** in **Settings**, under **Today**. Its open steps leave To do; the ones you have done stay done.

**To do** is the day's list. Each item says what it is ("Approve 4 drafts (Podcasts)", "Submit 2 live posts to Whop (Beast clips)", "Enter TikTok views for 3 posts", "Record 2 Whop decisions", "Reality drama ends tomorrow", "Payout ready on Whop", "1 post needs you", "Log in again: @handle on TikTok"), what it could earn or what is at stake ("a guess" when the rate or views are guessed), and about how long it takes. Its button (Review, Submit, Enter views, Record, Withdraw, Put up, Open the Queue, Open Accounts) takes you to where it is done and starts its timer. The item ticks itself when ClipForge sees the work done; you can also tick it by hand. An item left undone carries over to the next day, marked **Carried over**, and anything that comes up later in the day (a post that needs you, a login that expired) joins the list without a notification. **Done today** lists what is finished and whether ClipForge saw it done or you ticked it.

**Clips to review** holds the morning's drafts, grouped by clipping channel and then by video. All are ticked at first. Each plays in place, with its score, account, slot and caption (edit it right there), and, for a clip linked to a campaign, the campaign's rules as a checklist. **Approve** and **Discard** are on each card (Discard can be undone from its toast for 10 seconds). **Approve and post** approves the ticked drafts and opens the usual review step for them, and nothing posts until **Confirm and post** hands them to the publishers.

**Post** holds posts on manual export. **Open in TikTok** (or Instagram) copies the caption, opens the platform's upload page in your default browser and shows the file in Finder; **Mark as posted** closes it once it is up.

**Submit** has a row for each live post of a campaign. **Copy link and open Whop** (or the campaign's marketplace) copies the post's link and opens the campaign's submit page in your default browser (it is just **Copy link** until you save the campaign's submit page); paste it there and submit it yourself, then press **Mark submitted**. A link copied here never starts a project if it is pasted back into ClipForge. **Enter views** keeps the count you read on the post (until then its estimate says "No views yet"), and a row says when its 2-day or 7-day count is due. When the marketplace decides, **Approved** or **Rejected** records it.

**Guidelines** shows each active campaign's rules in short (rate, minimum views, most per clip, tags and mentions, what's not allowed, platforms, end date and budget left), and under them each account's posts today against its daily cap.

**Money** has a card per marketplace with what ClipForge estimates, what is pending and what you've recorded as paid. **Withdraw** opens that marketplace's payout page in your default browser (set the link in **Settings**, under **Marketplaces**) and is highlighted when something is ready. **I've been paid** fills in each approved post's amount from its estimate; change any to what actually came in and press **Save**, which closes the payout item.

**Yesterday's best** shows the 3 posts that gained the most views in the last day, from Instagram's daily read and the TikTok counts you entered, so you can see what works.

## How the estimates learn

Each kind of item starts with a guess: 20 seconds a draft, 15 seconds a post to submit, 10 seconds for each view count, decision or campaign ending, 30 seconds for a payout, and a minute for a post that needs you, a login, Instagram's insights permission or an exported post.

From then on, ClipForge times you. The timer starts when you press an item's button and stops when the item is done, however it gets done, even on another page: ClipForge closes the item the moment the work it waited on changes, without you coming back to Today. An item open for more than 30 minutes counts as left, not worked on, so it isn't timed. Once a kind of item has 3 timings, its estimate becomes the median time per draft or post of its last 20, so the list fits the way you actually work.

## What optional means

When the day's list would take more than 15 minutes, the items worth least are marked **Optional**, starting with the longest of those worth the same, until the rest fits. Items that can't wait are never optional: a post that needs you, a login, a view count that is due, a campaign that ends tomorrow and its posts still to submit. Optional items stay on the list; do them if you have time.

## The drafts cap

When a day's list runs over 15 minutes, the next day's drafts cap goes down by the drafts that didn't fit, at your own time per draft, and never below 1. The cap covers all your clipping channels together, and when it bites, the best drafts across them are kept. After 3 days in a row that left at least 2 minutes spare at the same cap, it goes up by one while your clipping channels make more than that in a day. Pausing channels never lowers it.

## The notification

ClipForge sends one notification a day, at the time in **Settings**, under **Today**, **Daily notification at** (7:30 am in the posting time zone unless you change it). It says what waits ("Your 15 minutes is ready: 8 drafts, 4 to submit, 1 payout"), leaving out the optional items, and clicking it opens Today. If the morning's drafts are still being made then, it waits up to 2 hours for them and then goes with what is ready. **Send the daily notification** turns it off.

Every other notification (a project ready, drafts made, a post that needs you, failed or went up) is off, and each of those shows on the To do list instead. **Notify me about every post, draft and project** turns them back on.

## TikTok's own schedule

A TikTok post that needs your click (assisted mode, or a sound to add) doesn't wait for its slot. During your 15 minutes ClipForge uploads it in the visible Chrome window with TikTok Studio's own schedule set to the post's slot, rounded to 5 minutes, and reads the date and time back. The banner then says "ClipForge has filled this in and set TikTok's schedule to" the time. Check it and press **Schedule** yourself, and TikTok publishes it at the slot. If TikTok shows a different time, nothing is scheduled: the window closes, so a press there can't hold the post at the wrong time, and the post needs you, naming both times. Press Retry to try again. A slot under 20 minutes away or more than 10 days ahead isn't scheduled this way; the post waits for its time as before.

**TikTok's own schedule**, in **Settings** under **Posting**, turns all this off. A TikTok post that needs your click then waits for its time and opens its window then, as before. Turn it off if TikTok Studio's schedule ever stops working, then press Retry on the posts that need you.

The Queue shows such a post as **Scheduled on TikTok**. About 10 minutes after its slot, ClipForge opens TikTok Studio's posts list once to record the post's link, uploading nothing. A post scheduled on TikTok can't be edited there, only deleted: to cancel or move it, delete it in TikTok Studio yourself. **Cancel** in the Queue only stops ClipForge following it.
