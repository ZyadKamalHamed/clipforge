# Briefs and account advice

Each morning ClipForge gathers the live campaign briefs it is allowed to read, works out what each could earn on your accounts, puts "Look at N new briefs" on Today's list in its place by that money, and suggests the accounts worth making for the money none of yours can reach yet. A brief is a lead, not a campaign: it becomes one only when you press **Use this campaign** and save, and you join it yourself on the marketplace.

## What ClipForge reads, and what it never does

ClipForge reads only what a logged-out visitor can see. It never logs into, joins, submits to or automates Whop, Vyro or any payout service, never reads anything behind their logins and never stores payment details. A site is read with plain GET requests, no cookies and no login, under an honest user agent that names ClipForge ("ClipForge/<version> (campaign brief reader)"). It keeps to the site's robots.txt (a site with none allows everything), to one request every 5 seconds (longer when robots.txt asks for it, up to a minute), to 40 brief pages a day, to pages under 5 MB, and to redirects that stay on the same site. A list that the site's scripts build is read in a headless Chrome page with a new, empty profile that is deleted afterwards, never an account's profile. That page keeps the same limits and can reach nothing but the site itself; the cookies the site sets while it loads stay in that profile and go with it. Every page's text goes to Claude as untrusted data, fenced off, with no tools, and its answer is checked against a fixed shape.

Whop's Content Rewards and Vyro are in the list, but their terms forbid automated reading (checked on 29 Oct 2026), so they are paste only and say so in Settings. Their campaigns still arrive through Campaign Scout's file, below.

The reading starts at 6:00 in your posting time zone, after the clipping channels' checks, and each source is read once a day. Claude fills in up to 200 briefs a morning. The morning's list waits for the reading to finish, up to two hours past the notification time. **Check now** in Settings reads one source again, at most once an hour, and never one that is paste only. After a site turned ClipForge away or showed a sign-in page, Check now waits for the next morning's read.

## Campaign Scout's file

HeyClicky's Campaign Scout already gathers public Whop and Vyro campaigns into a file on this Mac (`~/Library/Application Support/Clicky/projects/agents/campaign-scout/output/campaigns.csv`). It is the first source, on by default, and it makes no request to any site. ClipForge reads that one file and nothing else of HeyClicky's, and never writes to it. Each row becomes a brief keyed by its campaign link, so a campaign is one brief whichever source saw it. Campaign Scout's verdict and notes show on the brief as **Campaign Scout's notes**, apart from ClipForge's own estimate, which is never copied from the file. A row whose status says the campaign is closed or its budget is spent makes the brief gone.

If the file is missing, can't be read or has a new shape, this source stops for the day and a to-do item says what is wrong; the other sources carry on.

## Adding a site

In **Settings**, under **Brief sources**, **Add a site** takes a marketplace's name and its public list page (an http or https link). Whop, Vyro, Content Rewards and the other sites ClipForge only takes pasted briefs from are refused here. Read the site's terms first: ClipForge adds it only once you tick "I've read this site's terms and they allow automated reading". The site is then read each morning by the general reader: Claude picks the campaigns out of the list page, each one a link the page itself has, and then each new brief's own page is read in the list's order, within the 40 a day. A brief ClipForge already knows takes the list's new figures and keeps its text, so its page isn't read again, and one whose budget left is 0 is gone without its page being read. A brief page that is missing, sends ClipForge to another site, goes round in circles or that robots.txt keeps it from is left out, and the rest are read. Each source's row shows how it is read, its terms note and its last result, and its switch turns it off.

## When a site can't be read

When a site refuses ClipForge, asks it to slow down, shows a sign-in page, sends it somewhere else or has changed its pages, that source stops for the day, keeping the briefs it read before then. There are no retries in between and no way around a block is ever tried. Today gets a "Paste briefs from Clip Site" item, a paste box for that site appears under **New briefs** and on the Briefs tab, and the site is tried again the next morning. Paste a campaign's rules into the box and press **Make a campaign** to open the New campaign form filled from them, the same as Use this campaign.

## What a brief is worth

An account fits a brief when it is connected (or only its token is running out), not paused, on a platform the brief allows and tagged with one of the tags of the brief's niche. A brief is worth, a day, the best of its fitting accounts: that account's estimate for one post on the brief's rate and cap, times its daily cap, held to the budget left. A post's estimate is the share of the account's last 30 counted posts that reached the brief's minimum views, times the median 7-day views of those that did, divided by 1,000, times the rate. An account with fewer than 5 counted posts uses the starting guess (Settings, Today, **Starting guess for new accounts**, 1,000 views at 7 days), and the figure says "a guess". So a brief whose minimum views are above that guess shows about US$0.00 until your posts reach it. With no account fitting, a brief is worth one post at the starting guess on its best rate, also a guess; without a rate its worth isn't known.

Amounts stay in each brief's own currency. They are compared through the **Exchange rates to US$** in Settings, Today, and without one a non-US$ amount is compared as if it were US$ and marked so. Every figure is ClipForge's estimate, not the marketplace's.

## Today and the Briefs tab

"Look at 4 new briefs (about US$60 a day at your views)" joins Today's list when any new brief fits an account or a niche the advice suggests, sorted by that money like every other item, and says "2 of them reposting" when some are. **Look** takes you to **New briefs**, which shows the top 5 of them (reposting ones first, then the richest), each as on the Briefs tab, with **See all briefs** and **I've looked**, which marks the ones shown as seen. The item ticks itself once none is left.

**Campaigns**, then **Briefs**, has every live brief (new and seen), the reposting ones first and then the richest, with Niche and Marketplace filters.

**Reposting first.** A reposting campaign asks you to post its own ready-made videos (from an edit repository or a Drive folder) rather than cut clips from a long video. Those are the quickest money, so they head every list: the Briefs tab, New briefs, the could-take lines and the path's Pick your first campaign, richest first among themselves. Each wears a green **Reposting** tag. ClipForge counts a brief as reposting when its title, text or footage says repost, reposts, reposting or re-post, unless a word against it sits close by in the same sentence, before ("no raw reposts", "you can't repost") or after ("reposts are not paid", "reposted content will be rejected"). A brief that offers both clipping and reposting counts. Read the brief's rules before you post, as always. To post a reposting brief's videos as they are, open **Options** on Create and turn **Post as it is** on, then add the videos, as files or as the brief's Google Drive links (paste a Drive folder's link and each video directly in it, up to 50, becomes a project of its own; the file or folder must be shared as Anyone with the link): each one becomes a single ready clip, uncut, with no captions or hook title (a wide video is fitted to 9:16 over its own blurred frame). It takes videos up to 10 minutes long. Write each post's caption in the post dialog, then post and track it as usual. Turn the option off again before you cut clips from a long video. Each card has its rate, budget and the accounts it fits, with **Open** (its page in your default browser), **Use this campaign** and **Hide**. Below are the paste boxes for Whop, Vyro and any site that couldn't be read today. A brief that leaves its listing, or whose budget is spent, becomes gone.

## Niches

Briefs are sorted into the niches in **Settings**, under **Niches**, one a line with its language in brackets and its tags after the colon, such as `Gaming (en): gaming, games`. The list starts with reality TV and drama, gaming, streamers, podcasts and interviews, sports, comedy and entertainment, music, finance and business, tech, lifestyle and fitness, education, and news and politics. Claude picks each brief's niche from this list; a brief Claude didn't get to goes in the niche whose tags its brand, title and text name most. To cover a niche, give an account one of its tags in Accounts.

## Accounts worth adding

Under the new briefs, **Accounts worth adding** adds up the money on offer a day in each niche and suggests up to 3 with the most that no clipping channel of yours posts for. Each says why (how many live briefs, their budget left and the best 3), the accounts to make (a TikTok and an Instagram account, or only the platforms its briefs allow, and never TikTok for news and politics, which holds for the accounts it says could take a brief too), three name ideas, a bio, a picture idea, the clipping channel to set up and a posting plan within your caps. The names for the starting niches never pass as the show, network, creator or brand they clip, and the bio says it is a clips or fan page. For a niche you add, the names start with the first word of its name, so check them when that word is a show or a brand. Below the suggestions, **Your accounts could take today** names the briefs an account you have could take now.

ClipForge never creates or logs into a TikTok or Instagram account. Make the accounts yourself, add them in Accounts and tag one with the niche; then **Set it up** opens a New clipping channel filled from the suggestion, with the briefs' footage links as its sources, and the channel starts paused until you check it. **Not now** hides a suggestion, and **Not this niche** hides that niche in its language, both for 30 days.

## Using a brief and joining

**Use this campaign** opens the New campaign form filled from the brief (Claude reads its rules from the text alone, each filled field marked to check) and opens the brief's page in your default browser, when it is an https page on a marketplace ClipForge reads. A campaign or submit link Claude reads out of the text is kept only when it is that kind of link too. Tick that you've checked the filled fields and press **Save**. The brief becomes used, and Today gets an item such as "Join Beast clips on Whop", with **Open** (the campaign's page in your browser) and **I've joined**; the Campaigns sheet's triangle shows the same step. Joining is always your own click on the marketplace; press **I've joined** afterwards and the item and the line go away. Deleting the campaign makes its brief seen again, so you can use it once more.
